HTML Tags I Have Learned

<p>
Defines a paragraph of text.
<h1>
Defines the main heading on a page.
<img>
Displays an image on a webpage.
<a>
Creates a hyperlink to another page or website.
<ul>
Creates an unordered or bulleted list.
